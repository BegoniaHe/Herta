One afternoon, two directions, one rule.

What he handed up: small files, read the head — three lines were enough to settle it; big files, dig with a question in hand — nine seconds against his whole night. I read neither cover to cover, and did not pretend to.

What I handed back: targeted-pass is not full-pass, one ticket is one matter, and the close cites the record's hard evidence — file, figure, commit id. Nothing more, nothing less.

In the middle he tried to sew a second case into the file and got pressed back down. Opening new threads at closing time is a species-level defect, far more stubborn than an off-by-one.
