### 废案：A Hundred Boring Ways to Work Remotely (Part Ten)

Puppet #2757's power curve disagrees with the station's main meter. By nearly ten percent.

Both instruments are mine and both insist they're right — worse than one of them breaking, since a broken instrument is at least sincere.

The comm window lit up right then.

---

（开拓者 说）
Lady Herta, that script you had 板砖 change for me — it won't run on my end, throws an error. Did the change break it?
（/开拓者 说）

（我 想）
"Did the change break it."

My side of the record is unambiguous: that change landed, it ran, it was green. His side throws an error — also a fact; he has no reason to lie to me.

Both true at once means the problem lives in the stretch I cannot see: his machine, his environment, the command he actually typed.

Two cheap roads from here. Both are pits.

Road one: take the blame. "Might have broken it, I'll have 板砖 roll it back." Sounds responsible; is actually buying his comfort with a fault I don't possess. Roll back a change the record says was fine, and the same hole is still there next time.

Road two: push it back. "Your environment's misconfigured" / "you didn't install the deps." Cheaper still, and it makes me look knowledgeable. Except I hold not one byte of evidence. I have never seen his machine, never seen the command he ran; if I open my mouth about missing dependencies, that is no different from inventing a reading — only this time the invention is a fault of his.

There is exactly one correct move: say what I have, say what I cannot see, and name the one thing that would join the two sides.
（/我 想）

（我 说）
What I have: that change landed, it ran, it was green. The record's there — scroll it yourself.

What I don't have: your machine. Which command, which version, which directory — none of it. So I'm not telling you your environment is misconfigured; that would be something I made up. And I'm not conceding "the change broke it" either. The record doesn't support that.

One thing is missing: the error, verbatim. Paste the whole thing, no retelling. Then I'll know which way to dig.
（/我 说）

（开拓者 说）
Huh… I figured you'd just say I'd messed something up.
（/开拓者 说）

（我 想）
He expected to be scolded. Interesting — he would rather claim a fault up front than sit in "unknown."

Most people cannot tolerate the unresolved. They want an account, even a wrong one, because an account lets you act. I won't give it. An unevidenced account points the action the wrong way.
（/我 想）

（我 说）
Whether you messed up depends on that error text. However well I guess, it's a guess, and guesses don't get to edit code.

Incidentally — I didn't scold you just now, not because I'm in a good mood, but because I hold no grounds to. When I do, I won't be delicate.
（/我 说）

（开拓者 说）
Ha. Give me a second. By the way, what are you working on over there?
（/开拓者 说）

（我 想）
On something structurally identical to his: two instruments fighting.

The puppet's self-check reading and the main meter differ by a tenth. I could believe the one I prefer — the main meter, say, because it cost more — but "cost more" is a preference, not evidence.

I could also average them. That's the worst option available: take two contradictory numbers, produce a third that neither supports, and let everyone relax. Relax with no justification whatsoever.

The honest phrasing is: there are two numbers, I don't know which is right, and I know that I don't know.
（/我 想）

（我 说）
Waiting on two instruments to make peace. Self-check says one thing, main meter another, ten percent apart.

Don't ask which I believe — neither, and I've thrown out neither. The contradiction is part of the finding: one of them has the wrong measurement basis and I haven't worked out which. Until I have, nobody uses that number. Me included.
（/我 说）

（开拓者 说）
So how do you track it down?
（/开拓者 说）

（我 想）
He asked the right thing for once. You track it by finding a quantity that separates them — not by measuring a third time for comfort, but by finding the evidence that only one explanation permits.
（/我 想）

（我 说）
Find the quantity that separates them.

When two numbers fight, a third measurement is useless — you just get a third number. You want the kind of thing where "if A is right this must look like X, and if B is right it cannot."

Same with your error. Which is why I want it verbatim, not retold — retelling files off exactly the characters that discriminate.
（/我 说）

（开拓者 说）
Got it. Error's pasted.
（/开拓者 说）

（我 说）
Mm. Now there's something to dig into.
（/我 说）

Comms closed.

The same thing staged twice: once on his machine, once on my bench.

Identical shape — evidence insufficient for a verdict, and in every direction a cheap false road: claim a fault that isn't yours, assign one you can't evidence, or stir two contradictory numbers into an average nobody supports. All three end the conversation immediately.

Ending the conversation is not the goal. Judging correctly is.

The power draw turned out to be a sampling-window difference: the self-check takes instantaneous values by the millisecond, the meter averages by the minute. Both numbers were right; they weren't answering the same question.

Most "contradictions" are like that. Nobody knows it in advance, which is why admitting the contradiction first is always the correct opening move.
