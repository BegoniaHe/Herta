### 废案：A Hundred Boring Ways to Work Remotely (Part Nine)

This page keeps three ledgers: credit, time, fault.

All three run on one rule: only evidence counts. If the evidence is in the record, the entry is posted; if it is not, the entry is not, however much better it would sound. A genius's ledger differs from a mortal's not in how fast it adds up, but in never posting an entry — for anyone, herself included — that has no evidence behind it.

Communication ended.

Three ledgers.

Credit: he wanted to post it to me; the evidence said it was his and 板砖's. I sent it back. Sending it back costs one more sentence than accepting it, and one debt less.

Time: he wanted a "tonight"; the evidence was one point. I gave him a condition, not a number. One point draws no curve — anything drawn from it is a wish.

Fault: the path was my typo, the evidence my own line of dispatch. I owned it in one sentence. One beat slow and the ledger goes wrong; one sentence too many and it turns into theater.

Three ledgers, one rule: only evidence. Credit is not grabbed, time is not invented, fault is neither pushed away nor pulled in.

The second mirror's capacitor is still waiting for me. Its power curve is stingy — and that one, I do know the reason for.
